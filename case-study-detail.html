    Rethinking internationalisation for a digital university

    Bifröst University had fundamentally changed its teaching and learning model and moved all its degree programmes online. Its international relations had not kept pace with this change: the internationalisation strategy, the exchange models and some of the partnerships still dated from its campus-based days. The university’s leadership and international office wanted support to adapt strategy, exchange formats and partnerships to the new digital learning model.

    S03 · H2 sticky | 3 blocks (H3 · dash list / step list), lines between blocks

    My approach

    Goals of my consulting work

    • Rethink internationalisation for a digital university
    • Clarify which models of virtual student exchange would work
    • Develop recommendations for the future partnership strategy

    Method

    The work followed the three steps I use to shape change processes:

    1. Notice: interviews with those involved to understand the starting point and the differing expectations of internationalisation; a workshop with the leadership team in which we used design thinking to develop two personas, one for outgoing and one for incoming exchange students

    2. Align: 3D mapping to make the current state of international relations visible and develop a vision for the future

    3. Act with impact: recommendations for new exchange models and the future partnership strategy

    Result

    • The persona work brought to light a student profile that the university’s previous approach had overlooked, and it changed how Bifröst University thinks about its future exchange models.
    • Bifröst University has introduced new exchange models and established new international partnerships.
