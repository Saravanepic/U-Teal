    Who I am and how I think

    I’m Nadja Gmelch, founder of U-teal, a systemic higher education consultancy, working on internationalisation, strategy, organisational development and leadership across Europe and beyond.

    I love looking at familiar things from new angles and shifting perspectives. I help people shape change, in themselves and in their institutions. And I enjoy this most in the world I know best: universities, and the people who lead them.

    Universities are under pressure from several directions at once. AI is changing teaching, research and university management, budgets are shrinking, and society is asking more of them. Those who shape and lead universities are searching for new answers. How can we rethink universities so they can continue to serve society? And what kind of leadership will get them there?

    These questions are at the heart of my work.

    I’m interested not only in the what of change, but also in the how. What inner and outer conditions does change really need? How do institutions, and the people in them, find their own capacity for renewal? How can we activate collective intelligence so that something new can emerge? I believe that established leadership cultures and ways of working will not be enough on their own to meet the demands of our time.

    From 2007 to 2022, I worked inside higher education: as Head of Projects at the Association of Catalan Public Universities (ACUP) and as Director Europe and Open Knowledge at the UOC. Alongside these roles, I began consulting for universities in 2014. I know where and how universities make decisions: in the senate, in committees, in strategy processes. At the UOC, I led the creation of the OpenEU Alliance: I brought the partner universities together and prepared its first two applications to the European Universities Initiative. In 2024, the European Commission selected the alliance for funding. I have developed internationalisation strategies, built institutional partnerships and designed university-wide strategies for sustainability and open knowledge. Digital higher education is one of my core areas: at the UOC, one of Europe’s pioneers in online learning, I saw first-hand how digital learning changes universities, and I have published on the topic, including for the European University Association.

    From 2023 to 2025, I worked as a senior consultant at a strategic consultancy, mostly outside higher education. There I saw what becomes possible when organisations approach change systemically. I trained as a systemic coach and organisational development consultant (dacte). In the Presencing Institute’s Ecosystem Leadership Program, I learned awareness-based leadership and Theory U directly from Otto Scharmer. With U-teal, founded in 2026, I’m bringing all of this back to higher education: to the people who shape universities, and to the institutions themselves.

    I’m originally from Germany and have lived in Mexico City and Barcelona. Today, home is a small mountain village in the Apennines, Munich and wherever my camper van happens to be parked. I’m happy to work with you in English, German, Spanish, Italian or Catalan. I’ll come to you whenever your project needs it: for retreats, workshops and important meetings. Anything that works well online, we do online.

    How I work

    People and their development are at the centre of my work. I draw on Theory U, systemic coaching and systemic organisational development. My projects follow three steps: Notice, Align, Act with impact. They rest on three principles:

    Everything is connected

    I see universities as a web of people, committees, structures and relationships that all influence one another. That’s why I work on structures and processes and, at the same time, on the mindsets and inner stance of the people within them.

    Head, heart and body

    I bring together logical, emotional and intuitive intelligence, together with the body, which sends signals we often overlook.

    Prototypes, not recipes

    I help people design and test prototypes instead of handing them finished solutions. There is room to experiment: mistakes become learning opportunities, and a culture of learning takes root.

    Why U-teal?

    The U in U-teal stands for university, and for the U-shaped journey of Theory U. Along this journey, attention, intention and the capacity to act shift as we truly listen and allow something new to emerge.

    Teal is the colour between blue and green. For me, it stands for a third possibility in the space in between, for example between hierarchy and chaos. In his book Reinventing Organizations, Frédéric Laloux uses teal to describe a new kind of organisation: one built on self-management, distributed responsibility, wholeness and a clear sense of its purpose in society. This image guides my work. I’d like to see universities develop in this direction, each in its own way and at its own pace.
