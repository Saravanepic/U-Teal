    Project examples from my work in and with universities

    Rethinking internationalisation for a digital university

    Bifröst University in Iceland had moved to fully online teaching, but its internationalisation strategy still dated from before the switch. I helped the university’s leadership and international office develop new exchange models for digital learning and reorient its international partnerships. The university has since put both into practice.

    Read the full case study

    Strengthening international cooperation strategically

    The Fundació Autònoma Solidària is the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona’s foundation for social engagement and development cooperation. As project lead, I evaluated one of the foundation’s long-standing university partnerships together with a colleague. We examined what it had achieved, how lasting its impact had been and what could be transferred to other partnerships. The foundation has since used our recommendations to reorient its approach to international cooperation.

    Read the full case study

    Shaping the future of international research funding: programme evaluation for the DAAD

    Since 1994, PROBRAL has funded joint research projects between German and Brazilian research groups and the mobility of early-career researchers. For the programme’s 20th anniversary, I was part of joyn-coop’s evaluation team for the DAAD, working as an associate. The DAAD and Brazil’s CAPES have since implemented our recommendations for the programme’s future.

    Read the full case study

    Strengthening collaboration within a consortium: recommendations for a European-African funding network

    ERAfrica was an EU-funded network of European and African funding organisations that aimed to build lasting research cooperation in science and technology. I was part of joyn-coop’s evaluation team for one of the network’s joint calls, working as an associate. We looked at where planning, implementation and funding ran into difficulties and developed recommendations that laid the groundwork for the consortium’s further joint calls.

    Read the full case study

    International university partnerships: expert reviewer for Norway’s NORPART programme

    NORPART funds long-term academic cooperation and mutual student mobility between Norwegian universities and partner institutions in the Global South. As a member of the external expert panel, I assessed funding applications from university partnerships. Our assessments formed the basis for the funding decisions.

    Read the full case study
    S04 · H2 + 2 tiles (Loop Grid, group "Other sectors")

    Organisational and leadership development in other sectors

    Supporting a cross-sector network for Ukraine’s reconstruction

    The German Platform for the Reconstruction of Ukraine, an initiative of the German Federal Government, brings together companies, municipalities, civil society and academia to strengthen Germany’s contribution to rebuilding Ukraine. As a senior consultant at joyn-coop, I supported the platform’s secretariat with organisational development and process facilitation for over a year, on behalf of GIZ and the BMZ. This included agile ways of working for the distributed team, participatory network events, prototyping workshops and coaching. The platform has since introduced new formats and new ways of working together.

    Read the full case study

    Organisational change and leadership development for a European climate foundation

    A European climate foundation with teams in several countries set out to prepare itself for the years ahead in a “Fit for Future” process. As a senior consultant at joyn-coop, I supported the organisational development process, in which the foundation evolved its vision and values, culture, processes and decision-making together with its staff. I also developed a leadership programme to help its leaders put the new mission statement into practice. A large majority of the foundation’s staff took an active part in the process.

    Read the full case study
