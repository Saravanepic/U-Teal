Welcome to U-teal. I accompany women in higher education leadership and the universities they shape — with systemic consulting, coaching and sparring for tangible change.

The pressure on universities to change is growing. Polycrises, generative AI, skills shortages and demographic shifts demand more than adaptation. They demand that universities actively shape their own future.

I know the world of higher education from the inside. Together with you, I create spaces where a new quality of decision-making and action emerges.

Perspective

What makes a university future-making?

Not predicting the future, but gaining orientation for decisions under uncertainty — in three steps.

01

See clearly

Every change starts with genuine listening. Only those who pay attention to what is actually there can change it.

02

Align

What future does the university want to create? Once that intention becomes tangible, people carry it forward together.

03

Become effective

Intention turns into action — visible in decisions, in structures, in the everyday life of the institution.

Being future-making doesn’t come from occasionally thinking about the future, but from making future questions part of organisational routines — anchored in strategy, governance and organisational development. I think about shaping the future systemically, drawing on Theory U.
